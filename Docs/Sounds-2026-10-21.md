# Sounds — 2026-10-21

## User-facing behavior

- Sounds are synthesized in the app (no audio files) and are about 23 dB louder than before: messages and the ringtone sit near −15 dB, join and leave cues near −20 dB and control cues near −27 dB, so the important ones stand out. Sound-effects volume in Settings scales all of them.
- **Direct message:** a low two-note kalimba (E4 to G4), chosen to stay pleasant when messages arrive in quick succession. It also plays for channels set to notify for every message.
- **Mention:** a three-note bell, when someone @mentions you in a channel or thread.
- **Incoming call:** a double-burst trill that repeats every 2.6 seconds until you join or decline, for up to 45 seconds. It does not ring while you are in another call, in Do Not Disturb, or when you have muted the server. When the window is not focused and desktop notifications are on, a silent notification says who is calling.
- **Calling:** a soft ringback while you wait for the person you called, up to 45 seconds. **Call declined** plays when they decline.
- **Voice:** separate cues for you joining and leaving and for someone else joining or leaving the channel or call you are in. Each plays once per change.
- **Your controls:** mute, unmute, deafen and undeafen cues when you toggle them yourself, not when the app mutes you because of permissions.
- **Screen sharing:** cues when someone in your call starts or stops sharing. Shares already running when you join start silently.
- Settings: "Call connection sounds" is now **Call sounds** (joins and leaves, controls, sharing, ringback and declined). Incoming calls ring regardless of it. "Soft message notification sound" is now **Message notification sound**.

## Implementation

- `src/sounds.ts`: the catalog of fifteen sounds (notes as MIDI pitches with timing and decay) and `playSound`, which schedules each note's partials for its timbre (kalimba, bell or soft) through a short echo and a compressor.
- `src/notifications.ts`: `createNotificationSound` plays named sounds with a per-sound 700 ms burst limit, and rings looping sounds with `startLoop`/`stopLoop`, fading out on stop. It keeps output-device selection and the resume and shutdown guards. `notificationSound` chooses message or mention; `callRinging`, `callDeclined` and `callsRing` decide ringing.
- `src/main.tsx` rings and stops the loops from `directCalls` state and plays the declined cue. `src/useCall.ts` plays the join, leave, member, control and screen-share cues.

## Verification

- Sounds and ringing are deliberately not covered by the automated test suite. `npm run format:check`, `npm run check`, `npm test`, `npm run build` and `git diff --check` passed.
- Every sound was rendered offline in Chromium to measure its peak and loudness, with no clipping.
- Two Windows Electron clients against a temporary server, with oscillator frequencies recorded and each sound identified by its notes and timbre partials: a DM played the message sound and a mention the bell; an incoming call rang repeatedly while the caller heard the ringback; answering stopped both and played joined cues once; mute, leaving and someone leaving played their cues; a declined second call stopped ringing and played the declined cue on the caller's side; no renderer console errors.
