More flexible call views, clearer thread conversations, and a more compact, aligned messaging interface.

## Added

- **More call options** beside connection diagnostics, with two view toggles:
  - **Show participants without video** hides or shows avatar-only tiles while keeping cameras and screen shares available, including shares you have not started watching. Hiding a tile does not mute its voice audio.
  - **Grid View**, enabled by default, arranges participants together. Turn it off for a large selected participant above a row of avatar selectors; click an avatar to change the main participant. If the selected participant leaves or is hidden by the filter, another visible participant takes their place.
- **Expand call details** and **Expand conversation** buttons in the corresponding headers, available during a voice or private call. Each gives its panel the available workspace while keeping the other panel's compact header accessible. These buttons only expand; the divider remains available for custom sizing.
- **Mute and deafen badges** at the bottom-left of participant tiles. Deafen takes priority over mute, matching the indicators beside names in the voice-channel roster.

## Changed

- Participant and stream tiles now **fill the available call area**, replacing the previous fixed-size cap. Grid rows stay horizontally centered, including incomplete final rows. Video keeps its proportions without cropping, and controls retain a readable size.
- The bottom avatar strip in the selected-participant view is horizontally centered and scrolls when the participants do not fit in one row.
- Message composers are about **30% shorter when empty** and can grow to **224 px** before scrolling, up from 160 px. Attachment, emoji, and send icons stay aligned at the bottom as text expands.
- Threads show the **original message directly below the header**, separate from the scrolling replies. Its attachments, reactions, and message actions remain available; long originals have their own bounded scrolling area. Removed the old “Replying to…” line and the duplicate original message from the reply history.
- Thread search and pinned-reply controls now share the header with the title, reply count, and close button, leaving more room for messages.
- Moved server connection status below the server name, replacing the sidebar slogan with **Connected to server** or **Reconnecting…**.
- Tightened the voice-connection and profile panels, removed the redundant call-details button from the voice-connection box, and removed promotional footer text.

## Fixed

- Opening or reselecting a text channel, DM, or thread reply focuses the corresponding message input.
- Channel and thread composers, their buttons, and their helper text now align vertically when displayed together.
- Composer height updates when a draft changes programmatically or the available width changes, and shrinks again when cleared.
- The collapsed call section's resize divider aligns exactly with the sidebar server-header divider. The call and conversation expand icons also share the same horizontal position.
- Call toolbar spacing accommodates the extra options button in compact voice and private calls, including narrow layouts.
- Hiding avatar-only participants or switching the selected participant retains existing audio playback elements and individual volume settings.

## Upgrading

- Rebuild or update the desktop client to load the new interface and call controls.
- This release introduces **no database migrations or media-protocol changes** relative to the v0.1.8 source cutoff. Existing server data and permissions are unchanged.
- Call view options affect your own layout; they do not change other participants' layouts or stop their streams.
