Reaction notifications, clearer notification settings, Linux screen-share audio, and chat interface improvements.

## Added

- **Notifications for reactions to your messages.** Reactions from other people appear in persistent channel and DM badges, including reactions on thread replies. Sounds and desktop alerts follow your notification settings; self-reactions and reaction removals do not create alerts.
- **Reaction highlights when opening notifications.** A notification jump highlights the message and gives the matching reaction a short punch-scale animation. Other queued messages and reactions visible in the chat or thread are highlighted and cleared together; offscreen activity remains pending. Reduced-motion users receive a static highlight.
- **Linux screen-share audio.** After choosing a screen or window, share an application's audio, system audio excluding Campfire, or video only. Your microphone stays separate. See [Linux screen-share audio](../LinuxScreenAudio.md) for requirements and application-selection details.
- **Desktop update controls on the sign-in screen.** Supported desktop installations can check for and download client updates before connecting to a server.

## Changed

- Notification settings are grouped into Desktop notifications, Sounds, Server notifications, Channel overrides, Direct messages, and Reactions to your messages. Short descriptions explain which controls affect popups, sounds, or both.
- Reaction alerts have their own switch, saved to your account on this server and synced across devices. Turning it off preserves unread badges and notification jumps. Message levels use **All messages**, **Only mentions**, and **Nothing**.
- Dark and light themes now use neutral **Graphite** and **Paper** palettes, with updated backgrounds, borders, and role-color contrast. Existing dark, light, and system preferences are retained.
- Message times appear in a column before the avatar. Repeated times within the same minute remain hidden until hover or keyboard focus.
- Message hover actions are smaller and positioned higher above the message.
- **Maximum upload size (MB)** is now under **Attachment storage**, with its own save button.

## Fixed

- Channels and threads resume following incoming messages when you are at the bottom after a notification jump. Scrolling up preserves your reading position.
- Typing indicators appear above the composer in a reserved line, so they no longer move the input or other content when they appear or disappear.
- Message hover actions disappear when the pointer leaves after clicking a reaction, while remaining accessible with keyboard focus.

## Upgrading

- Update the server and clients together, rebuild and deploy browser assets, and restart the desktop app. Server updates remain manual. See [Desktop updates](../DesktopUpdates.md) for supported automatic client updates and manual package choices.
- Back up server data before updating. Existing v0.2.4 databases and read positions are retained; startup adds reaction tracking without replaying existing reactions. Reaction alerts start enabled for existing accounts and can be disabled in Notifications settings.
- Linux screen-share audio requires PipeWire and its PulseAudio compatibility service. Systems without the required support can still share video. Building the Linux desktop client requires the native dependencies listed in [Linux screen-share audio](../LinuxScreenAudio.md); browser-only builds do not.
